What Are Audien Hearing Aids — And Why Are More People Exploring Them?
Advanced Hearing Technology • Comfortable Design • Everyday Listening Support

Audien is a U.S.-based hearing aid company focused on affordability and simplicity. Their hearing aids are designed primarily for adults experiencing mild to moderate hearing loss, and many of their models fall under the FDA’s category of over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aids
. This means they’re available without a prescription and can be purchased directly online or at major retailers.

The brand also sells personal sound amplification products (PSAPs), which are different from medical-grade hearing aids. PSAPs are intended to amplify environmental sound for general use, such as watching TV or attending events, and are not approved for treating hearing loss. This distinction is important when choosing a device that matches your needs.

Audien hearing aids appeal to first-time users, budget-conscious shoppers, and those seeking an easy entry point into hearing technology.

Audien Atom™ — Frequently Asked Questions

Are Audien Hearing aids actually FDA-registered?

Yes. Audien devices are registered with the FDA, which allows them to be sold over-the-counter to adults with perceived mild-to-moderate hearing loss, without a prescription.

How much do Audien Hearing aids cost?

Prices generally range from about $99 to $489 per pair depending on the model—significantly less than the $2,000–$6,000 typical of clinically fitted hearing aids.

Are Audien Hearing aids good for severe hearing loss?

No. They’re built for mild-to-moderate hearing loss. Anyone with severe or sudden hearing loss should see an audiologist instead.

How do I set up my Audien Hearing Aid?

To set up your Audien Hearing Aid, start by charging it fully. Once charged, insert the device in your ear canal following the instructions provided in the manual. Adjust the volume to a comfortable level, and you're ready to go. If you need further assistance, visit our support portal.

Are Audien hearing aids covered by insurance?

While some insurance plans may cover hearing aids, we keep our prices affordable so you can purchase directly without insurance. We recommend checking with your insurance provider for coverage details.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, all Audien hearing aids come with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. We also offer a 45-day money-back guarantee.
